Privacy Policy — Aravel

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Aravel (“Aravel”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a classic books library application developed and published by Arsene Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Aravel mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

Aravel does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information in order to read books.

Information stored locally on your device

Aravel may store application preferences and reading-related settings locally on your device, such as theme preferences, font preferences, favorites, bookmarks, and reading progress. This information is used to provide the application's features and is not intentionally transmitted to us as a personal profile.

Advertising and device information

Aravel uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location information, ad interaction information, and other technical or usage information as necessary to provide, measure, protect, and improve advertising services.

The availability and use of personalized advertising may depend on your consent, applicable privacy laws, and the choices you make through the consent interface provided in the application.

2. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, Aravel uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy and consent choices. Depending on your location and applicable requirements, you may be asked whether you consent to certain uses of information for advertising and related purposes.

If consent is required and you do not provide the necessary consent, Aravel will not request advertisements when Google's consent system indicates that an ad request is not permitted.

You can manage available privacy choices through the privacy controls provided by Aravel and/or Google's consent mechanism when those controls are applicable.

3. Third-Party Services

Aravel uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies. These services currently include:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — used to display and measure advertisements.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — used to manage consent and privacy choices where applicable.

For information about how Google handles data when using its advertising services, please review Google's privacy documentation: Google Privacy Policy .

4. How We Use Information

Information handled by Aravel or its third-party services may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing and maintaining the Aravel application.
  • Saving application and reading preferences on your device.
  • Displaying and measuring advertisements.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and invalid advertising activity.
  • Complying with applicable legal and platform requirements.

5. Data Sharing

Aravel does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed or shared with service providers, including Google and its advertising partners, when necessary to provide advertising, consent management, security, measurement, or other services described in this Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Preferences such as favorites, bookmarks, reading progress, and display settings may remain stored locally on your device until you remove them, clear the application's data, uninstall Aravel, or the application removes or replaces the relevant data.

Aravel does not maintain a user account database for these reading preferences. Third-party services such as Google may retain information according to their own policies and applicable requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

Aravel is not specifically directed to children under the age required by applicable child privacy laws. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information under our control. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, services, legal requirements, or data practices change. When we make changes, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this page.
