Aravel: Classic Books Library

Support & Help

Welcome to Aravel

Aravel is a classic books library designed to make timeless literature easier and more enjoyable to read.

If you are experiencing an issue with the app, this page provides information that may help.

Reading & Reader

If you experience a problem while reading a book, please try closing and reopening the book or restarting the application.

Make sure you are using the latest available version of Aravel.

Bookmarks & Favorites

Aravel provides features such as bookmarks and favorites to help you keep track of books and reading progress.

If your saved information does not appear correctly, please restart the application and try again.

Ambient Sound

Aravel may provide ambient sounds such as rain, thunderstorm, forest, fireplace, ocean, or cafe ambience while reading.

If an ambient sound does not play correctly, check your device volume and make sure the application has not been muted by the device.

Appearance & Reading Settings

Aravel includes reading customization options such as appearance and font size.

If a setting does not appear to work correctly, restart the application and try again.

Ads & Consent

Aravel may display advertisements to support the continued development and maintenance of the application.

Depending on your location and applicable privacy requirements, you may be asked to provide or manage consent for personalized advertising and related processing.

Privacy

For information about how Aravel handles personal data, advertising, privacy choices, and related services, please review our Privacy Policy.

View Aravel Privacy Policy

General Troubleshooting

  • Make sure you are using the latest version of Aravel.
  • Restart the application.
  • Restart your device if the problem persists.
  • Make sure your device has sufficient storage space.
  • Check your internet connection when an online service is required.

Need More Help?

If you continue to experience a problem, please contact our support team and include as much useful information as possible about the issue.
