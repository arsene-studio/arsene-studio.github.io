About Aravel

Aravel: Classic Books Library is a reading application created to make classic literature accessible in a simple and enjoyable way.

Aravel focuses on classic works that are available in the public domain, allowing readers to discover and enjoy timeless literature.

Our Purpose

The purpose of Aravel is to provide a comfortable reading experience for people who enjoy classic literature.

We aim to combine the character of classic books with a modern reading experience that is simple, accessible, and enjoyable.

Classic Literature

Aravel features classic literary works that are available in the public domain.

Public-domain works can be freely accessed and enjoyed because their copyright protection has expired or they are otherwise available for public use under applicable laws.

We strive to provide appropriate source and publication information for the works included in the application.

Reading Experience

Aravel includes features designed to make reading classic books more convenient and enjoyable.

  • Classic literature collection
  • Comfortable reading interface
  • Adjustable reading appearance
  • Adjustable font size
  • Bookmarks and favorites
  • Reading progress
  • Ambient sounds for reading
  • Highlighting of selected archaic words
